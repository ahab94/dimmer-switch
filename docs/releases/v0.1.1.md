**Sound!** Dimmer Switch now has music and sound effects, including in the browser.

- An original thriller theme in two layers: the tense strings always play, and the drums and brass swell in as guards get suspicious.
- Suppressed shots, shattering bulbs, takedowns, night-vision whine, alarms, keycards, objective beeps, guards going "huh?", and quiet footsteps (softer when sneaking than running), duct crawling and ladder rungs.
- Every sound is synthesized in code; no samples.

In the browser, sound starts after your first tap or key press (browsers block sound until then).
