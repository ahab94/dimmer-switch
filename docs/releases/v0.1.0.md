First release of **Dimmer Switch**, a side-view light-and-shadow stealth game.

▶ Play free in your browser (phones too): https://ahab94.github.io/dimmer-switch/

- 10 missions, each with an objective and a short briefing: hack, photograph, bug, steal, and finally flip the master switch for a full blackout.
- Light and shadow decide everything: shoot out lamps, hide in dark doorways, crawl through air ducts, use night vision to see lasers.
- Guards, flashlight guards, cameras, lasers, keycard doors, takedowns.
- Touch controls on phones and tablets (joystick plus USE, SHOOT and NV), with portrait and landscape layouts.
- Every mission is verified winnable, unseen, by the autopilot on every build.

Downloads below: Windows, macOS (universal), Linux and a web build. Or install in one line; see the README.
